Squash Tracker is a match companion for squash players, built around scoring a
game live on Apple Watch. Each rally is logged as it happens — server, side, and
outcome — so the score, the game, and the match update themselves rather than
needing a manual tally kept elsewhere.

Playing a match also starts an Apple Health workout session, so heart rate and
active energy are captured for the same window the score was recorded in and
shown alongside it afterwards. A companion iPhone app carries match history,
player and court management, and statistics — win-loss records, game breakdowns,
and milestones — synced across devices via CloudKit.

Squash Tracker's active development concluded with a v2 schema rewrite in April
2026; racket-sport tracking continues in a successor app, Rallying, with an
export/import path for carrying match history across.
