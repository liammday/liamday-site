title: v1.1 — DoBIH catalogue widening
summary: A second UK hills database is folded in alongside OpenStreetMap.
---
The peak catalogue widened to include the Database of British and Irish
Hills alongside OpenStreetMap, with cascading import, pre-matched merges
against the existing mirror registry, and UI to surface DoBIH-only peaks
inside downloaded collections. Getting two independent data sources to
agree on the same hill without duplicating it turned out to be a bigger
job than the map rendering that came before it.
