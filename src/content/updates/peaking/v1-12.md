title: v1.12 — One layout system, and every list behaves the same
summary: Lists, tiles and cards all render through a single engine, and each section remembers how you like to see it.
---
Every list, tile grid and card shelf in the app now draws through one
layout engine instead of several that had drifted apart. In practice that
means the margins, the rounded corners, the swipe actions and the
long-press menus are the same wherever you meet them — and a long press
lands on the thing you actually pressed, which in grids it did not always
do before. Each section also remembers its own view: peaks can be a list
while trails are cards, rather than one switch flipping the whole app.
Sort and view controls moved into a single control on each section, so
there is one place to look. Away from the layout work, the weather
timeline now covers a full day rather than a clamped window, and searching
for a hiking list by name finds the collection.
