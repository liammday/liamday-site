title: v1.3 — Strava as a primary source
summary: Strava graduates from optional extra to a primary way of confirming a summit.
---
Strava import was promoted from a secondary signal to a primary source
alongside HealthKit for the Friends & Family beta, which meant tightening
up route deduplication so activities recorded a year apart no longer merged
into one. A collection-structure rubric was also introduced and enforced at
import time, codifying rules for which sub-peaks belong inside a list versus
standing alone — a decision that had previously been made by hand, list by
list.
