title: v1.4 — Grouped achievements and searchable settings
summary: Achievements gain hierarchy, and Settings becomes searchable rather than browsed.
---
Achievements grew a parent/child structure so a family of related peaks
could roll up into one meta-achievement, with a recursive evaluator and a
dedicated peakingctl command to publish the hierarchy to CloudKit. Settings
also stopped being something you scrolled to find: a search catalogue and
pure matching engine were built and wired into every screen, including the
developer tools, on the view that a growing app needs a way to find its own
switches.
