title: v1.5 — Making friend invites actually reliable
summary: Connecting with a friend gets a typeable fallback and self-service recovery.
---
Friend invites had been fragile — a share link that failed silently, or a
connection that got stuck with no way to tell why. This cycle added a
typeable short-code as a fallback to the tappable CKShare link, plus
visible connection status and self-service repair when a sync stalled.
Underneath, several CloudKit zones needed a legacy-keyed rehydration path
so existing connections survived the change rather than quietly breaking
for anyone who had already added a friend.
