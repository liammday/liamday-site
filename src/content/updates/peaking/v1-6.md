title: v1.6 — A Photos tab and real-time friend activity
summary: Photos become a first-class content type, and friend activity starts to feel live.
---
A new Photos tab unified photo feeds from every source into one gallery,
with clustering on the map, cross-links back to the route or summit a
photo was taken on, and deep links from a peak straight to photos taken
there. Friend activity moved from a static list to something closer to
real-time: notifications on a logged summit or earned achievement, plus a
fix so removing a friend actually revoked their CloudKit access straight
away rather than on the next sync.
