title: Fixing "Me" in score announcements, and more flexible match setup
summary: Announcements learn to say your name properly, and match creation gets faster with remembered defaults.
anchor: naming-and-match-options
---
A recurring naming bug in the spoken announcements — the app referring to
the player as "Me" instead of their name, producing lines like "Me's
service from left" — was traced to a first/third-person mix-up and fixed
properly rather than patched around. Match setup became more flexible
soon after, in early July: a "best of" target became optional (falling
back to an open-ended match), and new-match options now prefilled from
whatever rules, target, location and ball type were used last, so
starting a second match took noticeably fewer taps.
