Rallying started as a new Xcode multiplatform project, immediately
followed by a straight port of the v3.A foundations from Squash Tracker:
the slot-based schema, the shared iPhone UI (matches, players, locations,
settings), the Watch recording surface, and the CSV importer that lets
existing testers carry their history across. The sport picker was gated
to the squash family for launch, so the app shipped as squash-only on
day one even though the schema underneath was already multi-sport.
