title: "v1.0: every sport scored properly, singles or doubles, and a way home for SquashTracker players"
summary: The first external TestFlight build, with real rules for all eleven sports, doubles, and a first-launch import of SquashTracker history.
---
Rallying went to external testers for the first time. Every sport in the
picker now has its own rules rather than a generic first-to-N score:
English and point-a-rally squash, racquetball side-out, badminton and
table tennis rally scoring, pickleball side-out or rally scoring, and full
tennis scoring with sets, deuce and tiebreaks, which padel, paddle,
platform and beach tennis share, with golden point where the sport uses
it. Doubles arrived on top of the same model, so scores and results stay
per side while the app works out which player serves next, down to
pickleball's three-number call. The first launch now asks whether you are
coming from SquashTracker and imports that history in one step, which
matters because those players are the reason the app exists. Getting the
build out also meant giving Rallying the TestFlight details the other
apps already had, so it could clear Beta App Review at all.
