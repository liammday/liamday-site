Text-to-speech announcements were added so a score update could be heard
rather than read mid-rally, with a toggle to turn them off and several
rounds of fixing audio clashes and crashes along the way. In parallel, the
statistics side of the app matured well past a simple win-loss count:
combined stats-and-milestone views, sortable and filterable match lists,
and a trend indicator showing whether a given stat was rising or falling
over recent matches.
