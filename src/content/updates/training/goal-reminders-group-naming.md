Activity and nutrition goals were connected to Reminders as
progress-bearing entries, with a matching link for sleep goals to
CloudKit shortly after. In parallel, group-name representation was made
consistent across every screen that displayed a workout, with "also known
as" aliases merged into the same gazetteer used for natural-language
disambiguation — so a workout typed one way and a workout selected from a
picker resolve to the same name.
