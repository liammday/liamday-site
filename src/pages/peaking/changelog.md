# What's new in v1.8

Peaking v1.8 introduces seamless background synchronization for your activities and brings your friends' climbs to the shared map.

## Highlights
- **Background Strava Import**: Peaking now passively imports activities in the background. GPS tracks from Garmin, COROS, Wahoo, Suunto, and Polar watches sync automatically via Strava without needing to open the app.
- **Shared Friends Map**: Friend summits can now be plotted directly onto the shared map, providing a unified view of where your climbing partners have been.
- **Customizable Detail Views**: Peak and collection detail screens are now fully customizable. Sections can be reordered or hidden entirely to prioritize the information you care about most.
- **Browse Layout Adoption**: The new Browse layout has been rolled out across Summits, Routes, Friends, and Achievements, offering a consistent and flexible way to explore your data.
