## Common questions

### A walk is missing, or has no route

Peaking needs the GPS route, not just the workout. Apple Watch and most iPhone recording apps save the route to Apple Health. Garmin, Polar, Suunto, COROS and Wahoo do not, so connect Strava or import the GPX, TCX or FIT file instead. Check too that Peaking has permission to read workouts and workout routes in the Health app.

### A summit I reached was not counted

Peaking counts a summit when your recorded route passes close enough to the top. A route with a gap near the summit, or a peak that is missing from OpenStreetMap, can be missed. You can always log the summit by hand from the peak's page.

### My data is not on my other device

Peaking syncs through your own iCloud account. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Peaking. A workout's GPS route stays on the device that recorded it, so another device shows the route's details but not its line on the map.

### Adding a friend

Open Friends and share an invite link or code. Each link or code works once, for one person. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

### Strava

Connect or disconnect Strava from Settings, then Permissions & Connected Services. Disconnecting deletes the data Peaking imported only from Strava.

### Deleting your data

Delete routes, summits and connections from inside the app. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. To remove the iCloud copy too, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage, then Peaking.

## Try it without a recorded walk

Download [a sample walk up Snowdon](/peaking/snowdon-llanberis.gpx) on your iPhone or iPad, then in Peaking go to Routes, tap +, choose Import file and pick it. Peaking finds Snowdon (Yr Wyddfa) on the route and logs the summit.
